# ADR 0001: Yjs behind CrdtDocument

Status: accepted  
Date: 2026-09-08

## Decision

Use Yjs 13 as the first production CRDT, hidden behind `CrdtDocument` in `@jot/core`. Yjs types must not escape `packages/core` and `packages/editor-web`.

## Why

Hermes has no comfortable WASM path. Yjs is JavaScript, has a mature ProseMirror binding, and maps structured rich text onto `Y.XmlFragment`. Loro and Automerge remain Phase 0.5 benchmarks, not v1 anchors.

## Consequences

Tombstones and retained history grow. Native editor bindings are custom. Compaction and user-visible versions are separate features, not free CRDT side effects.
